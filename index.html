Workflow

From the field to your dashboard in 4 steps

Our streamlined workflow ensures data moves seamlessly from field collection to actionable intelligence with zero friction.

01
Collect in the field
Field teams use the mobile app to capture environmental readings, complete structured surveys, and attach photos with automatic GPS tagging.
02
Sync to Google Sheets
Data is automatically pushed to your connected Google Sheet in real time. No manual exports, no CSV uploads, just live always-current data for your team.
03
Analyze and visualize
The dashboard automatically generates trend charts, spatial heatmaps, and statistical summaries. Drill down by region, date range, or survey type instantly.
04
Report to stakeholders
Generate professional, branded PDF reports with a single click or schedule automated delivery to keep everyone aligned without manual effort.
